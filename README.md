1. Site Overview
Apna Punjab is a vibrant and informative website designed to promote Punjabi culture and local businesses in Gravesend. The website provides users with information on activities such as learning Punjabi, music, dance, sports, and martial arts, while also featuring local food stores, restaurants, and fashion outlets. The goal is to create a central hub where residents and visitors can easily find events, businesses, and opportunities related to Punjabi culture.

2. Planning Stage
2.1 Target Audience

The primary audience for the site includes:

- Local residents of Gravesend with a connection or interest in Punjabi culture.

 - Tourists looking to explore Punjabi activities, businesses, and cultural experiences.

 - Local business owners interested in advertising their Punjabi-related services.

2.2 User Stories

As a user, I want to find local Punjabi classes, music, and dance events so that me and my family can participate and learn more about the culture.

As a visitor, I want to find Punjabi restaurants and food stores in the area so that I can enjoy authentic cuisine.

As a business owner, I want to advertise my Punjabi business on a dedicated website that targets my ideal audience.

As a user, I want to be able to contact the website admin to inquire about events or advertising.

2.3 Site Aims

To serve as a go-to resource for all Punjabi-related events, activities, and businesses in Gravesend.

To create a visually appealing and user-friendly website that reflects the rich culture of Punjab.

To support local businesses by providing them with a platform to advertise their services.

To encourage community engagement through events, classes, and workshops.

2.4 How This Will Be Achieved

The site will feature intuitive navigation and vibrant visuals reflecting Punjabi culture.

Dedicated pages for "Things To Do" and "Local Businesses" will help users find relevant information quickly.

Contact forms and advertising submission forms will allow for user engagement and business promotion.

Regular updates will keep users informed about new events, classes, and local opportunities.

2.5 Wireframes (Used Balsamiq)

Wireframes were created using Balsamiq to map out the basic layout of each page. These wireframes focused on clear sectioning, intuitive navigation, and the inclusion of vibrant images to enhance user engagement.

2.6 Colour Schemes
The site incorporates vibrant and culturally relevant colours such as:

Orange (#ff4500) and Yellow (#ffcc00) for energy and warmth.

Royal Blue (#4169e1) and Navy Blue (#000080) for a regal, calming effect.

Pink (#ff69b4), Green (#008000), and Red (#ff0000) for a lively, diverse palette. 

These colours represent the vibrancy and celebration within Punjabi culture and are incorporated into backgrounds, text, and section dividers.

VS Code as the primary code editor.

3. Current Features Common to All Pages

3.1 Header Element
i. Title:
The site title, "Apna Punjab - All Things Punjabi In Your Local Area," is prominently displayed in the header, making it clear to users what the website offers.

ii. Logos:
Placeholder logos or images related to Punjabi culture are incorporated into the header to enhance visual engagement.

iii. Navigation Bar:
The navigation bar contains links to the main sections of the website: "Home / About," "Things To Do," "Local Businesses," and "Contact Us." It is simple and user-friendly.

iv. Punjabi Prints and Colours:
The vibrant and culturally significant colours, such as yellow, orange, and royal blue, are used in the header to reflect the celebratory and festive aspects of Punjabi culture.

3.2 Footer

The footer includes contact information and placeholder links for social media accounts (Instagram, Facebook, Email).
The footer uses a deep orange and purple colour scheme to provide contrast while maintaining the site's vibrant aesthetic.
Social media icons are provided for future Instagram, Facebook, and email accounts.

4. Future Enhancements

Newsletter Feature: Implement a subscription service to inform users about upcoming events and business promotions.

Event Calendar: Add an interactive calendar on the "Events" page where users can view upcoming events and register.

Reviews Section: Allow users to leave reviews for local businesses listed on the site, helping others make informed decisions.

---------Enhanced Mobile Responsiveness: Ensure the site is fully optimized for all devices, including smaller screens and tablets.

E-commerce Functionality: In the future, local businesses may be able to sell products directly through the website, supporting the local economy.

Dynamic Content Management: Move to a content management system (CMS) to allow easy updating of event and business listings without needing direct code changes.
